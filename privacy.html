PRIVACY POLICY

Your notebooks are yours

Effective October 30, 2026

In short: Moju does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Moju has no servers and does not send any information about you or your notebooks anywhere. We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and we do not use any third-party code that collects data.

Your notebooks, photos and drawings

  • Notebooks, pages, photos, videos, stickers, text and drawings are stored only inside Moju on your device.
  • Moju can only see the photos and videos you choose in the photo picker. It cannot browse your photo library.
  • When you add a photo or video, Moju keeps a copy of the image itself, or a short silent clip of the video. Sound and hidden details such as location are not kept.
  • Your device may include Moju’s data in its regular backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your own device settings. These backups are handled by Apple, not by us.
  • Deleting Moju from your device deletes your notebooks.

Permissions

Moju asks for permission to add photos to your library only when you save a page as an image or video. It never asks to read your library. You can change this at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Moju Pro is sold through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Sharing

A page leaves your device only when you choose to share or save it, using the destination you pick.

Children

Moju does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If Moju ever starts collecting data (for example for an optional sync feature), this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to moju.app.help@gmail.com.
